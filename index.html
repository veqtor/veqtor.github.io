Tekniska museet, Yellofier & Synchroid

Where sound design, mobile development and public installation met

"Musik med hela kroppen" — an interactive music installation developed for Tekniska museet (the National Museum of Science and Technology) in Stockholm. Yellofier — the Android version of the sample-sequencing music app (originally created with Yello's Boris Blank). Synchroid — a sequenced Android synthesizer with a professional synth engine, built under the Veqtronix name during the Interactive Sound Design studies.

Stockholm · b. 1984 — son of jazz saxophonist Nisse Sandström

Began making music in the mid-90s, growing up around the playing and composition of his father, the renowned Swedish jazz tenor saxophonist Nisse Sandström (1942–2021). Recognised on Swedish Wikipedia as one of very few published Swedish breakcore & drum-and-bass artists.
